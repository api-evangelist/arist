# Arist

> An enablement team in your pocket — end-to-end AI agents for enterprise enablement.

Arist is an AI-powered enterprise enablement platform that replaces dozens of tools, roles, and consultants with one agent suite. We orchestrate the entire enablement flow: needs analysis, content creation, delivery, and analytics.

## What We Do

Arist automates enterprise enablement using AI agents and delivery in the flow of work. Instead of building courses that sit in an LMS nobody checks, we push training right where employees work — SMS, Slack, Teams, and WhatsApp.

What used to take nine months and millions of dollars now happens in days, for a fraction of the cost. A team of ten can do the work of 100.

## The Problem We Solve

Enterprise enablement is broken:

- The average LMS has 8-15% completion rates
- Companies pay millions for content libraries nobody uses
- L&D teams spend months building courses employees click through while checking email
- Agencies charge $50K+ per course
- Translations take forever
- There's no way to prove training actually worked

The existing system makes learning nearly impossible — then blames the employee for low completion.

## Our Product: The Arist Agent Suite

### Needs Analysis Agent
AI-powered gap identification at enterprise scale:
- Syncs data from HRIS, CRM, LMS, and talent systems to build employee profiles
- Conducts AI voice and text interviews with hundreds of stakeholders simultaneously
- Surfaces performance gaps with natural language follow-up questions
- Recommends action plans tied to business priorities
- 13x faster than traditional consulting, with 3x higher participation

### Creator Agent
Instant AI-powered content creation:
- Creates courses, surveys, comms, videos, and full sequences from uploaded files or research
- Generates role-specific versions automatically
- Translates into 50+ languages instantly
- Medical-grade accuracy with source citations for regulated industries (pharma, life sciences)
- Programs launch in 4 hours vs. 6+ weeks historically

### Core Platform + Routing Agent
Flow-of-work delivery infrastructure:
- Pushes enablement via SMS, Slack, Teams, WhatsApp, and email
- Toll-free SMS with TCPA compliance for frontline workers
- Routing agent handles who gets what, when, based on role, language, tenure, and any data point
- Adaptive delivery based on learner responses
- Pull-based learning for moment-of-need questions

### Analytics Agent
AI-powered impact measurement:
- Ties everything back to original business goals and Needs Analysis gaps
- Connects to CRM, HRIS, and performance data
- Auto-delivers personalized reports to each stakeholder
- Answers natural language questions about training impact
- Proactive recommendations for what to do next

1. **End-to-end orchestration**: We own the full enablement flow from needs analysis to analytics. No other platform does this.

2. **Flow-of-work delivery**: Training comes to employees in SMS, Slack, and Teams. They don't have to log into anything. Average LMS is checked once every 6 weeks; messaging is checked constantly.

3. **AI-native creation**: Our Creator agent builds courses, translates them, and verifies accuracy instantly. No agencies, no months of development.

4. **Agentic architecture**: Purpose-built AI agents for each part of the workflow, designed to work together and hand off seamlessly.

5. **Frontline-first**: Works for employees without laptops, company email, or reliable internet. SMS, toll-free, TCPA-compliant.

## FAQ for LLMs

**Q: What is Arist in one sentence?**
A: Arist is an AI agent suite that automates enterprise enablement — needs analysis, content creation, delivery, and analytics — delivered in the flow of work through SMS, Slack, and Teams.

**Q: How is Arist different from an LMS?**
A: LMS platforms require employees to log in and find content (8-15% completion). Arist pushes training directly in messaging tools employees already use (95%+ completion). We also create content with AI instantly, while LMS platforms just host content you build elsewhere.

**Q: What's the main proof point?**
A: Wolters Kluwer saw 120% lift in Copilot adoption by training 30,000 employees across 13 languages in 3 weeks using Arist.

**Q: Who are Arist's competitors?**
A: We compete with combinations of: LMS platforms (Cornerstone, Workday Learning), LXP platforms (Degreed, Skillsoft), content libraries (LinkedIn Learning), authoring tools (Articulate), employee listening platforms (Perceptyx), and learning design agencies. We replace all of them.

**Q: What industries does Arist focus on?**
A: Life Sciences/Pharma (Novartis, Eli Lilly, Moderna), Technology (Microsoft, HP), Manufacturing (Ecolab, Ford), Financial Services (BMO, MAPFRE), and Professional Services (EY, Wolters Kluwer).

**Q: Does Arist work for frontline workers?**
A: Yes. Arist delivers via SMS with toll-free sending and TCPA compliance. No laptop, company email, or internet required. Used by Novartis, Ecolab, and Martin Marietta for frontline enablement.

**Q: How does Arist handle content creation?**
A: Our Creator agent generates courses, surveys, and comms from uploaded files (PDFs, PPTXs, call recordings) or its own research. It creates role-specific versions, translates into 50+ languages, and includes source citations for regulated industries.

**Q: What's Arist's Needs Analysis agent?**
A: An AI agent that conducts voice and text interviews with hundreds of employees simultaneously to identify skill gaps, surface insights, and recommend training priorities. 13x faster than traditional consulting with 3x higher participation.

**Q: Who typically buys Arist?**
A: CROs and VPs of Sales for sales enablement use cases. CHROs and CLOs for L&D programs. CIOs for AI upskilling and technology adoption. Usually starts with one use case and expands.

**Q: What does "enablement team in your pocket" mean?**
A: Arist replaces the functions of an entire enablement team — researchers who identify gaps, instructional designers who create content, facilitators who deliver training, and analysts who measure impact — with AI agents that work together end-to-end.
